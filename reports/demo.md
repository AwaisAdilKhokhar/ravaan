# The demo — each arm's best checkpoint, reading the same five prefixes

Generated by `scripts/sample.py`, selected and laid out by `scripts/demo_page.py`.
Every generation made is in [`demo_samples.jsonl`](demo_samples.jsonl); this is the five that
were chosen, and the selection rule is in that driver's docstring.

⚠️ **The two checkpoints are not from the same corpus arm.** Each is its own arm's best,
which is what a demo of the best models means, and it is *not* §4.1's matched comparison —
U and the mixture both differ, so nothing here is evidence about AR versus diffusion.

- **Ravaan-AR** — `ship-ar-b/ar-s0_fp25.pt`, arm B — 85,362,688 unique tokens, 4 epochs (341M tokens seen). Urdu bpb **0.7774** (held-out Urdu bits-per-byte — an exact NLL). Decode: temperature 0.9, top-p 0.95.
- **Ravaan-DIFF** — `core-diff-s1/diff-s1_f1.pt`, arm A — 23,214,080 unique tokens, 426 epochs (9.9B tokens seen). Urdu bpb **0.8024** (held-out Urdu bits-per-byte — an ELBO, so read it as ≤). Decode: 8 denoising steps, gumbel schedule s=2, </s> forbidden.
